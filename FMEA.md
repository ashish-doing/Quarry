# QUARRY — Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA)

Severity (S): 1 (negligible) – 5 (safety/data-loss critical)
Likelihood (L): 1 (rare) – 5 (frequent, seen in testing)
Risk = S × L. Mitigation column states what's actually built, not aspirational.

| # | Component | Failure Mode | Effect | S | L | Risk | Mitigation (built) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Robot control | Control process crashes or loses connection mid-drive | Robot keeps moving uncommanded | 5 | 2 | 10 | Pi's own 0.5s movement watchdog — verified by killing the control process mid-drive |
| 2 | Vision | Object very close to camera (potential collision) | Robot drives into obstacle | 5 | 3 | 15 | `CollisionGuard` — independent async loop, bbox-area proxy, 0.15s check interval, 30% frame threshold, runs in both `inner_agent.py` and `site_agent.py` |
| 3 | Vision | `CollisionGuard`'s own frame read fails | Guard silently stops checking | 5 | 2 | 10 | `try/except` around frame read; loop continues on next tick — **known gap: a sustained read failure gives no operator alert**, logged at debug level only |
| 4 | Vision | False-positive "person" detection (clothes on furniture, etc.) | Wrong candidate raised, OR a spurious intruder alert fires | 2 | 4 | 8 | Dedicated `"clothing"` label competes against `"person"`; per-label confidence floor (0.65 vs 0.45 default) — same mitigation now protects both the candidate pipeline AND the intruder-alert path, since both read from the same detections list |
| 5 | Vision | Stationary registered object re-raises as new candidate every cooldown window | Sightings panel floods | 2 | 5 | 10 | 25s cooldown + "don't raise if one's already pending" check |
| 6 | Vision | Single-frame flicker misdetection | Candidate raised on a fluke | 2 | 3 | 6 | Temporal smoothing — label must appear in ≥3 of last 5 ticks |
| 6b | Vision | Object is real but tiny/at frame edge when smoothing clears | Candidate raises on a poorly-framed capture — a bad proof photo | 2 | 3 | 6 | `MIN_FRAME_FRACTION` capture gate in `maybe_raise_candidate()` — waits rather than raises until the bbox area clears a minimum threshold |
| 7 | Re-identification | OSNet backend fails to load (no CUDA, missing weights) | Targets never auto-match by appearance | 3 | 2 | 6 | Degrades to `available = False`; falls back to manual voting only, does not crash the feed |
| 7b | Re-identification | `register_mission_objects.py` run on one machine, `inner_agent.py`/`site_agent.py` run on another without copying `target_gallery.pkl` | Every candidate raises as unregistered — no OSNet match ever fires, and by extension no OCR cross-check either (it's gated on `is_registered`) | 3 | 3 | 9 | `load_gallery()` logs plainly at startup whether a gallery was found; `register_mission_objects.py` prints per-object registration status and refuses to claim success if `save_gallery()` fails. **Real gap: nothing currently verifies the loaded gallery's target names actually match `objects_config.json`'s** — a stale gallery from a different object set would silently produce zero matches with no specific error pointing at the mismatch. |
| 8 | OCR | Tesseract binary not installed (common on fresh Windows setups) | OCR cross-check silently never runs | 2 | 3 | 6 | `MarkerOCR.available` gates all use; `get_tesseract_version()` checked once at load, logged clearly with the Windows-specific fix (UB-Mannheim build) rather than a generic pip-install suggestion that wouldn't actually fix it |
| 9 | Identity logic | OCR reads a different number than OSNet's match implies | Real anomaly — object may be mislabeled, misplaced, or OSNet mismatched | 3 | 2 | 6 | Flagged explicitly (`ocr_anomaly`) in the sightings panel and proof gallery, never silently resolved either direction — a human sees it and decides |
| 10 | Identity logic | OCR has no signal this frame (bad angle, glare, nothing readable) | Could be mistaken for evidence something's wrong | 1 | 4 | 4 | `cross_check()` explicitly returns `None` for "no signal," same as "agreed" — only a genuine number mismatch is ever flagged. Dedicated test coverage (`test_sequential_survey_components.py`) locks this behavior in. |
| 11 | Mission logic | Inner UGV can't reach a waypoint (obstacle, bad heading estimate) | Mission stalls on one object indefinitely | 3 | 2 | 6 | `MAX_TICKS_PER_TARGET` hard ceiling in `inner_agent.py` — surfaces as a skipped-target log line, moves on to the next object rather than hanging the whole survey |
| 12 | Mission logic | Object confirmed by spectators before OCR/OSNet actually agree, or never confirmed at all | Proof gallery has gaps, or contains a disputed identity that was pushed through anyway | 2 | 3 | 6 | Voting is independent of the OCR/OSNet signals — spectators see both and decide; this is by design (human-in-the-loop is the point), not a bug, but it does mean the proof gallery reflects human judgment, not just automated agreement |
| 13 | Multiplayer | Two players claim the same name | Identity collision, unclear attribution | 2 | 2 | 4 | Case-insensitive uniqueness check at join, rejected with clear reason |
| 14 | Multiplayer | A player spams votes/pings/chat | Server load, panel spam for others | 2 | 3 | 6 | Rate limiter — 8 actions / 5s per connection, server-enforced |
| 15 | Multiplayer | A disputed candidate never resolves | Sightings panel accumulates permanently-pending items | 3 | 3 | 9 | Symmetric dispute-rejection threshold — `net_confirms <= -DISPUTE_THRESHOLD` triggers `match_rejected`, same as the confirm path |
| 16 | Alerts | Intruder alert fires repeatedly for the same lingering person | Alert log floods, real signal gets buried | 2 | 3 | 6 | 15s cooldown in `check_intruder()` — deliberately shorter/looser than the candidate cooldown, since alert timeliness matters more than dedup here, but still bounded |
| 17 | Alerts | Outer UGV raises an alert but no inner UGV is currently connected | `inner_note` in the alert message is silently blank | 1 | 2 | 2 | `hub.inner_site()` returns `None` gracefully, message just omits the inner-position note rather than erroring — the alert itself still broadcasts |
| 18 | Alerts | Image payload attached to a candidate is large (high-res crop, slow connection) | WebSocket send lags, other messages queue behind it | 2 | 3 | 6 | `PROOF_IMAGE_MAX_DIM` downscale (480px) + JPEG quality 80 before base64 encoding, applied in both `inner_agent.py` and `site_agent.py` — a real bound, not aspirational |
| 19 | Data flywheel | Crop save fails (bad image, disk full, permissions) | Training data silently incomplete | 2 | 2 | 4 | `try/except` around `cv2.imwrite`; failure printed, does not crash the relay loop |
| 20 | Data flywheel | Fine-tune run against too little data | Overfit, meaningless accuracy delta reported with false confidence | 3 | 3 | 9 | Hard gate — refuses below `MIN_SAMPLES_PER_CLASS` (8) or `MIN_CLASSES` (2) |
| 21 | AprilTag pipeline | Pose estimated without real camera calibration | Plausible-looking but wrong distance/position shown during confirmation | 4 | 2 | 8 | `apriltag_detector.py` refuses to run without `camera_intrinsics.json` present |
| 22 | Waypoint measurement | Robot physically can't reach a tag (blocked, or deliberately visible-but-unapproachable for layout) | That waypoint has no drive-and-center measurement | 2 | 2 | 4 | `measure_waypoints.py` prints exactly which waypoints weren't recorded this session at exit — an explicit gap list, not a silent one. No automated fallback exists; unreachable tags need manual tape measurement, by design (the alternative, fusing AprilTag's camera-relative reading with `get_pose()`, needs yaw, which this project doesn't compute) |
| 23 | Digital twin sync | `set_pose()` write fails | Cyberwave platform's own twin view goes stale | 1 | 2 | 2 | `try/except`, logged at debug, does not break local telemetry |
| 24 | Multi-site | A Field Agent (inner or outer) disconnects mid-mission | Their site's candidates/telemetry vanish for spectators | 2 | 2 | 4 | `site_removed` broadcast on disconnect, cleanly removes the site |
| 25 | Voice control | Short-word misrecognition ("back" → "thank you") | Wrong command executed | 3 | 3 | 9 | Whisper prompt-biasing toward known command vocabulary — reduces, does not eliminate |
| 26 | Navigation | Directed point-to-point steering | Wrong-direction drive from a bad yaw guess | 4 | — | — | **Deliberately not implemented anywhere** — quaternion→yaw conversion left unverified rather than guessed, in both `inner_agent.py`'s nav controller and `measure_waypoints.py`'s measurement method |
| 27 | Video/remote | Remote spectator can't reach a same-machine-only annotated feed | Feature works locally, appears broken remotely | 2 | 5 | 10 | **Known, undocumented-as-fixed gap** — needs ngrok/tunnel, not yet built |

## Highest-risk open items (Risk ≥ 9, not yet fully mitigated)

- **#2 (Risk 15)** — CollisionGuard is a coarse bbox-area proxy, not true depth sensing. A hard hardware ceiling, not a software gap.
- **#3 (Risk 10)** — no operator-facing alert if CollisionGuard's own frame reads start failing silently.
- **#7b (Risk 9)** — no cross-check between a loaded gallery's target names and `objects_config.json`'s expected names. A real, closeable gap: `RealRobotState.__init__` could log a warning listing any `objects_config.json` target_name that has zero embeddings in the loaded gallery, catching a stale-gallery mistake at startup instead of silently producing unregistered detections all mission.
- **#15 (Risk 9)** — has a real mitigation already built (symmetric dispute resolution); listed because the underlying failure mode is still possible, just handled rather than silent.
- **#20 (Risk 9)** — has a real mitigation already built (data-volume gating); same reasoning as above.
- **#25 (Risk 9)** — voice misrecognition is honestly bounded, not solved. Acceptable given voice control is lower-priority than the CV/safety/mission core.
- **#27 (Risk 10)** — same-machine-only video is a known, real gap with no workaround built yet.
